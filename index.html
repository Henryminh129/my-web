Services

End-to-End Personal Brand Services

Brand lives in story, trust, and what makes you unmistakably you. Every service below exists to protect that story. I help you through the shoot, the edit, and the strategy about around your raw footage, working 100% remotely with clients anywhere in the world.

↗

Channel Strategy

I help you build a content roadmap based on real audience data, defining a direction that grows sustainably while staying true to who you are.

↗

Video Production & Post

From editing to color, sound and pacing, animation, annotation. Every video is finished to fit the standards, holding attention and emotional impact to the very last second.

↗

Thumbnail & Title Optimization

I test and optimize thumbnails and titles against real CTR data, helping your video stand out among thousands of daily suggestions. But, honestly, this is not my core specialty.

↗

Personal Brand Building

I define a consistent visual identity, voice and story across every platform - from YouTube to TikTok and Instagram. Learn more: Brand Identity

↗

Growth Strategy

I analyze data, optimize posting schedules and content formats with one goal: your actual outcome — more sales, more leads, or a reputation people trust.

↗

Remote Shooting Guidance

I don't hold the camera - But I can instruct you on framing, lighting and delivery, then build the strategy around your own raw footage. Fully remote, built for clients anywhere in the world.

↗

Brand Partnership Consulting

I connect and advise on brand collaboration deals, making sure every partnership fits your image and maximizes revenue for your channel. Learn more: Partnership Rates

Bonus — By Request Only

Paid Media & Ads Management

For clients who want one person accountable for the whole channel, I can also step in to run and manage paid ads on top of the content system I've already built for you. I'll say this plainly: it isn't my core specialty yet, so I offer it as an optional extra, not a promise, and I'll tell you honestly if it's not the right fit for your channel.

I only bring it up because I care about your actual results, not just the content (it's because I do care about direct response).
